// learning journal

Designing AI adoption for the enterprise

A 6-week, ~1 hour/day program on how to design AI adoption inside an organization — written from the perspective of someone building on top of existing models: agents, skills, plugins, scripts, and marketplaces, rather than training models from scratch. Each week below is a working record of what I learned, with examples, diagrams, and notes filled in as I go.
